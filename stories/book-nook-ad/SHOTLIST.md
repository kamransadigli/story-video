# Book nook ad: what to film

Film vertical (9:16), daylight from a window, phone on a stack of books or a tripod.
Every shot shows the real kit. No claim goes in the script that the footage does not show.

| Shot | File | What to film | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 hook | clips/01-hook-shelf.mp4 | Finished nook between books on a shelf, lights on, slow move toward it | 3-4 s |
| 02 | photos/02-box-contents.jpg | Box contents laid flat on a table, from above | photo |
| 03 | clips/03-build-timelapse.mp4 | Phone timelapse of your hands building (record the whole build, keep 4 s) | 4 s |
| 04 | clips/04-details-closeup.mp4 | Very close: tiny furniture, books, signs inside | 3 s |
| 05 | clips/05-light-on.mp4 | Hand presses the switch, lights come on (keep the click sound) | 3 s |
| 06 | clips/06-final-shelf.mp4 | Wider shot of the shelf with the nook, slow pan | 3-4 s |

Render: `python3 -m storyvideo stories/book-nook-ad --out book-nook.mp4`

Before posting: if the kit does not have a switch, or is not wood, change shots 02/04/05 and their narration.
