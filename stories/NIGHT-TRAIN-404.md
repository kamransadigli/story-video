# Night Train 404 — series bible (keep every part consistent with this)

## Hidden truth (revealed in Part 3)
- Clara Weiss (29) is a witness who was about to testify against a smuggling ring.
- Her "husband" in seat 14, Jonas Weiss, is not protecting her. He works for the ring and was escorting her to them.
- She planned her escape with the conductor, an old friend:
  - he left her name off the passenger list ("she never boarded"), so no one could prove she was on the train;
  - the "ticking" in her suitcase is an old alarm clock set to ring at 00:31, when the train slows to walking speed over the bridge works. Everyone crowds round the suitcase, and she slips out of the rear door.
- The letters "HE" on the fogged window are the start of "HE'S NOT MY HUSBAND", interrupted.
- Twist: the man who raised the alarm is the danger. The missing woman saved herself.
